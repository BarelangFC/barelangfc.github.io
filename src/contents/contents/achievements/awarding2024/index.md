Politeknik Negeri Batam held an Appreciation Night for Outstanding Students and Lecturers, which took place at the Auditorium on Thursday, December 12, 2024.
<br>
<br>
The event began with a Mini Expo showcasing student project and competition products from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM (WIB). The expo was accompanied by a live band performance by KUAS (Kumpulan Anak Seni), a student arts organization at Polibatam, and included a product review session hosted by the event’s MC.
<br>
<br>
The main evening program started at 7:00 PM, beginning with a prayer and Qur’an recitation by outstanding students, followed by opening remarks from the Director of Polibatam, Mr. Ir. Bambang Hendrawan, ST., MSM., CIPMP., CISCP. The event continued with the award presentation to students and lecturers for their achievements in national and international competitions, interspersed with a Kempo performance by an award-winning student and another musical presentation by KUAS Polibatam.
