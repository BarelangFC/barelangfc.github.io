POLIBATAM – A Remarkable Achievement by Politeknik Negeri Batam at the National Industrial Robotics Competition 2025
<br/>
<br/>
Politeknik Negeri Batam (Polibatam) once again achieved an outstanding accomplishment at the prestigious National Industrial Robotics Competition 2025 (NIRC 2025) held on Monday, February 21, 2025. In this competition, Polibatam proudly won the Overall Champion title, proving the quality and competitiveness of its students in the field of technology and robotics at the national level.
<br/>
<br/>
The Polibatam team achieved an extraordinary victory by winning several prestigious awards, including:
<br/>
<br/>
Overall Champion of NIRC 2025

🏆 1st Place in the main competition category

🥈 2nd Place in Best Industrial Standard Assembly and Control Precision category

🥈 2nd Place in Robotic Programming category
<br/>
<br/>
This success is the result of the hard work and dedication of the students, as well as the unwavering support and guidance from their supervising lecturers. Amidst challenges of efficiency and limitations, Polibatam remains committed to continuously enhancing its students’ competence and competitiveness at both national and international levels.
<br/>
<br/>
Polibatam Director, Bambang Hendrawan, expressed his deep appreciation for this achievement.
"Alhamdulillah, this accomplishment shows that Polibatam students are capable of competing in the fields of robotics and industry, aligning with our vision to produce graduates who are ready to face the challenges of Industry 4.0. Congratulations to all the students, mentors, and every supporter, both directly and behind the scenes, who continue to support Polibatam’s excellence," he stated.
<br/>
<br/>
The National Robotics Industrial Competition is an annual event participated in by leading universities across Indonesia. The competition aims to sharpen students’ skills in robotics, industrial automation, and control systems.
<br/>
<br/>
With this victory, Polibatam further strengthens its position as a leading vocational education institution that continues to innovate and contribute to technological development. It is hoped that this achievement will serve as motivation for other students to keep innovating and achieving excellence in the future.
<br/>
<br/>
Congratulations to the Polibatam Team on this remarkable accomplishment!
