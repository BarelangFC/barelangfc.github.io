Politeknik Negeri Batam held an Appreciation Night for Outstanding Students and Lecturers, which took place at the Auditorium on Tuesday, December 12, 2023.
<br>
<br>
The event began with a Mini Expo showcasing student competition products from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM (WIB), accompanied by a live band performance by KUAS (Kumpulan Anak Seni), a student arts organization at Polibatam, as well as a product review session by the event's MC.
<br>
<br>
The main evening program started at 7:00 PM, beginning with a prayer and Qur'an recitation by outstanding students, followed by opening remarks from the Director of Polibatam, Mr. Uuf Brajawidagda. The program continued with the awarding of outstanding students and lecturers, interspersed with a Kempo performance by an award-winning student and a band performance by KUAS Polibatam.
